# Bachelor thesis: Enhancing Digital Identity

**Aligning DigiD with the eIDAS Regulations and EU Data Act** (TU Delft, BSc Technology, Policy and Management, Information & Communication track, July 2024).

Report: [`bachelor-thesis-digid-eidas-report.pdf`](bachelor-thesis-digid-eidas-report.pdf)

## Research question

Can the EU Data Act be implemented in the Netherlands even if there is uncertainty about whether DigiD complies with the Dutch Wet Digitale Overheid (WDO), which is based on eIDAS?

## Approach

A mixed-methods study: a literature review of the Data Act, eIDAS and the WDO, expert interviews (DigiD, eIDAS policy, national identity data, a security professor and a project manager at the Ministry of the Interior), and insights from the Identity Week Europe conference.

## Main findings (from the report's summary)

- The Data Act needs secure identification and authentication, which eIDAS regulates, so DigiD and the planned digital wallet have to meet the WDO requirements for citizens to share their data securely.
- The high security level required to activate a digital wallet depends on ID documents and driving licences with the right chips, and their slow rollout can delay broad adoption by years.

## What I learned

- **Regulation is a system.** EU rules are written separately, and a country has to fit them together into one working legal and technical whole.
- **Interviews add what the literature cannot.** Talking to people who work with DigiD, eIDAS and the WDO shows how the rules are applied in practice.
- **Writing a long research report.** Splitting one big question into sub-questions, planning interviews with a standard list of questions, and keeping a clear line from findings to conclusion.
