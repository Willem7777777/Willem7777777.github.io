# Digital Platform Design (TU Delft MSc CoSEM, SEN1641)

- `battcycle-individual-report.pdf` - individual report: high-level design of BattCycle, a fictional digital platform for battery sharing and repurposing in the EU (stakeholders, platform architecture, governance, and business model).
- `data-spaces-group-report.pdf` - group report: comparative analysis of the data spaces BDI, EONA-X, DSTM and SCSN, with a literature-based analytical framework. Group work, written with three other students.
