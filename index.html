Research

MSc thesis – Privacy threats in MCP and A2A

TU Delft, Complex Systems Engineering and Management · 2026 · Read the thesis in the TU Delft repository

Research on privacy threats in the API protocols for AI agents (MCP and A2A), with financial fraud detection at banks as the use case. The core question: how can systems communicate securely and in a standardised way on top of existing APIs? The work modelled the workflow end to end, mapped the threats against an AI privacy framework and validated them in expert interviews. Followed by further exploration of agentic payments: how do you authorise and verify a payment that an agent initiates on behalf of a user?

BSc thesis – Enhancing Digital Identity: Aligning DigiD with the eIDAS Regulations and EU Data Act

TU Delft, Technology, Policy and Management · July 2024 · graded 8.3/10 · Read the report (PDF) · Notes

The EU issues regulations that are developed separately, and each country has to fit them together in a working digital and legal system. This project asks whether the EU Data Act can be implemented in the Netherlands without DigiD fully complying with the Dutch Wet Digitale Overheid, which builds on eIDAS. Using a literature review, expert interviews and insights from the Identity Week Europe conference, it concludes that DigiD must meet the WDO requirements for citizens to share their data securely under the Data Act, and that the slow rollout of ID documents with the required chips is a further delay.
